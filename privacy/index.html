Privacy

A small site. A clear account.

This notice describes the public website as it works today. The planned academy will need a separate notice before collecting student information.

Last updated 16 November 2026

The current public site

Public pages can be read without an account. Invited contributors can sign in to write and submit articles. There is no public registration, student enrollment, checkout, or payment collection. The website does not add advertising or audience analytics.

Contributor accounts and drafts

Contributor accounts use an email address, a password managed by Supabase, a public byline, and an access role. Account sessions use essential secure cookies. The owner must also use an authenticator app. Draft text, revision information, submission status, and upload records are stored in the website’s Supabase database. Uploaded files and publication copies are stored in Cloudflare R2.

Contributors can access their own drafts; the owner can review all drafts and manage contributor access. Drafts and their attachments become public only when the owner publishes them. Account email addresses are not displayed with published articles. Choose a public byline and review text, captions, and media before publication. Downloaded or cached copies held by readers cannot be recalled by unpublishing.

Saving and account email

The writing desk saves recovery copies of unfinished edits in this browser’s local storage. A successful server save clears the corresponding recovery copy. Use your own device and clear site data before giving it to someone else. Resend processes the recipient address and message for invitations and password-reset emails. These account emails are not a mailing-list subscription.

Theme preference

If you choose a light or dark theme, the site can save that choice in this browser's local storage so your preferred appearance returns on a later visit. This is a display preference, not a visitor profile. You can remove it through your browser's site-data settings.

The Gaussian process explorer

The explorer calculates in your browser. Points you add are held in the page's memory and reset when the page reloads. The site's code does not send those points to a server or save them in browser storage.

Hosting and technical requests

Cloudflare serves the website and uploaded files; Supabase provides accounts and the database; Resend delivers account emails. These providers process technical information needed to provide their services, such as network addresses and request information. Account endpoints also use a hash of the network address to limit repeated attempts. See the privacy notices for Cloudflare, Supabase, and Resend.

Keeping and removing information

Drafts and uploaded files remain until removed by the owner; revoking contributor access does not delete their work. Contact the public address below to request an export, correction, or removal of your contributor information. Service backups or operational records may have separate retention periods. Keep your own original media files and copies of important writing.

Email contact

Email links ask your browser to open your configured mail application. Clicking a link does not send a message. If you choose to email the public address, your message and the details you include are handled through the email services involved. Please share only what is needed for your inquiry.

The future academy

Student accounts, subscriptions, private course materials, and forums are planned features. They are not active on this public site. Before student information or payments are collected, the academy will need its own privacy information describing the services used, the information required, and how it is handled.

Questions

For a question about this website, email Carl_Fr_Gauss@proton.me.

At a glance

The essentials.
