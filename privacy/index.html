Privacy

A small site. A clear account.

This notice describes the public website as it works today. The planned academy will need a separate notice before collecting student information.

Last updated 9 November 2026

The current public site

This is a public static website. It has no visitor accounts, enrollment system, checkout, payment collection, or submission forms. The website's own code does not add analytics or set cookies.

Theme preference

If you choose a light or dark theme, the site can save that choice in this browser's local storage so your preferred appearance returns on a later visit. This is a display preference, not a visitor profile. You can remove it through your browser's site-data settings.

The Gaussian process explorer

The explorer calculates in your browser. Points you add are held in the page's memory and reset when the page reloads. The site's code does not send those points to a server or save them in browser storage.

Hosting and technical requests

Your browser requests the pages and assets needed to display this site. GitHub, the hosting provider, may process technical request information, including your IP address. Its handling of that information is described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Email contact

Email links ask your browser to open your configured mail application. Clicking a link does not send a message. If you choose to email the public address, your message and the details you include are handled through the email services involved. Please share only what is needed for your inquiry.

The future academy

Student accounts, subscriptions, private course materials, and forums are planned features. They are not active on this public site. Before student information or payments are collected, the academy will need its own privacy information describing the services used, the information required, and how it is handled.

Questions

For a question about this website, email Carl_Fr_Gauss@proton.me.

At a glance

The essentials.

  • Public pages without sign-in.
  • No analytics or cookies added by the site's code.
  • An optional theme preference saved in your browser.
  • Explorer points stay in page memory.
  • Hosting providers may process technical requests.
