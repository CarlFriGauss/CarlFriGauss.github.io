Academy / Statistics

Academy · Field 03

Statistics

Learn to ask what data can tell us, and what a conclusion depends on.

In development

A proposed direction

Follow the reasoning from data to inference.

These are proposed areas of study. The course structure, prerequisites, and lesson sequence are still being developed.

Data & the question

How observations are obtained, what is being measured, and how the question shapes an analysis.

Models & assumptions

Statistical models, their assumptions, and the relationship between a useful simplification and the data it describes.

Estimation & uncertainty

Estimators, sampling variation, and ways of expressing the uncertainty around a numerical answer.

Inference & interpretation

Evaluating evidence, comparing explanations, and communicating conclusions with their conditions and limits.
