The teaching approach

A board. A question. Time to work.

The academy is planned to bring recorded lessons and live, interactive teaching into the same course experience.

Live classes are intended to use a digital writing board so that an argument can unfold one step at a time. Students will be able to ask questions, and teachers will be able to teach with their camera off.

Each course is intended to have its own materials, recordings, and discussion forum, where students can compare approaches and receive instructor replies.

Some lessons are intended to be freely available as previews. The paid teaching platform, subscriptions, and student accounts are still being planned.

A few practical details

Before the first course.

When can I enroll?

The academy is in development. There is no enrollment date or subscription offer yet. Course details will be published here when they are ready.

Will there be free lessons?

Yes, the plan includes selected free lessons so that you can experience the teaching before deciding whether to subscribe. No academy lessons have been published yet; the Gaussian process explorer is available now.

Will I be able to ask questions?

The planned format includes interactive live classes and a separate forum for each course, with student discussions and instructor replies.
