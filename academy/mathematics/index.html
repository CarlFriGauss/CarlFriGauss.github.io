Mathematics

A precise language for structure, change, and the arguments that connect them.

In development

A proposed direction

From an example to an understanding.

These are proposed areas of study. The course structure, prerequisites, and lesson sequence are still being developed.

Reasoning & proof

Definitions, logical structure, and the role of examples and counterexamples in building a mathematical argument.

Functions & change

Functions, limits, derivatives, and integrals, with attention to the ideas that make the notation meaningful.

Linear structure

Vectors, transformations, systems of equations, and the geometry behind linear algebra.

Connections & abstraction

Recognizing shared structure across problems and learning when a more general point of view becomes useful.
